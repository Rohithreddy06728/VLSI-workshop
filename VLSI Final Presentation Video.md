# VLSI Training – Final Presentation Video

**Presenter:** B. Rohith Reddy  
**Branch:** Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)

## About This Video

This video presents the knowledge and practical work completed during my VLSI training. It covers the major stages of digital IC design, from RTL design and simulation to physical implementation and static timing analysis.

## Topics Covered

1. **RTL to GLS** – Verilog RTL design, simulation, synthesis, and gate-level simulation.
2. **Physical Design** – Floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, and physical implementation.
3. **Physical Design and Global Routing** – Physical implementation stages and routing evidence.
4. **Static Timing Analysis (STA)** – Multi-corner timing analysis using OpenSTA and SKY130 timing libraries.
