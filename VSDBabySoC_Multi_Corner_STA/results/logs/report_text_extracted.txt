1. What I Did
I performed static timing analysis on the synthesized VSDBabySoC design using OpenSTA 3.1.0. I first checked the
available files and libraries, debugged the initial linking problem, loaded the SKY130 standard-cell timing library,
linked the synthesized netlist, applied the 11 ns clock constraint, and then ran setup and hold checks across the
available 18 timing-library cases.

The evidence section intentionally includes the terminal screenshots with the Ubuntu/VirtualBox desktop visible, the
commands I entered, warnings that appeared during the work, and the final completion message. The raw-output
appendix contains the copied terminal logs rather than replacing them with only a cleaned-up summary.

3. STA Setup Used
The clock constraint used for the working analysis was:
set_units -time ns
create_clock -name clk -period 11 [get_pins {pll/CLK}]

The normal OpenSTA sequence used for each corner was:
read_liberty /data/Desktop/VSDBabySoC/src/lib/avsdpll.lib
read_liberty /data/Desktop/VSDBabySoC/src/lib/avsddac.lib
read_liberty <corner SKY130 HD .lib>
read_verilog /data/Desktop/VSDBabySoC/src/lib/vsdbabysoc.synth.v
link_design vsdbabysoc
read_sdc /data/Desktop/VSDBabySoC/src/sdc/vsdbabysoc_synthesis.sdc
report_checks -path_delay max -group_path_count 1 -format full_clock_expanded
report_checks -path_delay min -group_path_count 1 -format full_clock_expanded
report_wns
report_tns
report_clock_skew

During early runs, the standard-cell library was missing and OpenSTA created black boxes for many SKY130 cells.
After loading the appropriate SKY130 HD Liberty file, the design linked and timing paths became available. The
recurring default_fanout_load warning was retained in the raw evidence because it is part of the actual terminal
output.

8. What the Results Show
The recorded FF and TT cases have non-negative setup-path slack in the final dataset. The SS cases show negative
setup slack, with the largest recorded setup limitation at ss_n40C_1v28.

       Item                                        Recorded result

       Clock period                                11.00 ns

       Most negative WNS                           -62.56 ns at ss_n40C_1v28

       Most negative TNS                           -13957.59 ns at ss_n40C_1v28

       Hold slack at that corner                   +1.83 ns

       Representative normal setup path            +1.94 ns (MET)

       Representative hold path                    +0.31 ns (MET)

       Final execution                             ALL 18 CORNERS COMPLETED


PVT behavior in this run is limited to the synthesized design, 11 ns constraint and Liberty data used here. The SS
cases in the lower-voltage conditions produced the negative setup results recorded in the table, while the FF cases
retained positive setup-path slack.
